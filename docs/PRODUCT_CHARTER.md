# Primer Peldaño - Product Charter v2

**Charter date:** 29 October 2026  
**Venture owner:** David Buzali  
**Stage:** Week 12 conditional rebuild  
**Decision source:** Week 12 Method, Verdict, three-founder Blueprint, and David's PPPP-RRRR-VVVV Manifesto

## 1. Purpose

Primer Peldaño exists to make useful informal work legible for one real job opening without allowing software to rank a person or pretend that evidence is verified. It advances David's commitment to opportunity and educational inequality by testing a concrete labor-market barrier: an employer may reject capable people because their experience came through an unfamiliar or uncertified workplace.

## 2. Exact user and consequential moment

The first user is an experienced worker in Mexico whose useful work is difficult for an employer to interpret because it lacks familiar credentials, titles, or institutional provenance. The consequential moment occurs **before** the worker invests time preparing evidence: the employer must first publish the criteria for one opening and accept named evidence types in place of the rejected pedigree proxy.

The reported October 2026 story is need evidence, not public product data. Primer Peldaño will not publish the worker's name, story, documents, or evidence until consent and the employer gate are documented.

## 3. Product promise

> For one opening, show what evidence the employer agreed to consider, map worker-controlled evidence to each declared criterion, preserve provenance and missingness, and reopen gaps for correction - without a global score, hiring prediction, or automatic verdict.

## 4. Core feature for Week 12

The Week 12 build is a **criterion-level evidence map** with an employer-commitment gate.

1. The opening declares four criteria and acceptable evidence types.
2. The interface displays whether the commitment is real, simulated, or absent before evidence entry.
3. The worker chooses an invented demonstration record, edits three bounded evidence fields, and controls what is shown.
4. Deterministic rules map visible statements to each criterion as `supported`, `partial`, or `unresolved`.
5. Every non-empty result cites visible provenance; an unresolved criterion explains what is missing.
6. The worker can correct the input and export a local, portable record.

## 5. Non-negotiable conditions

- **Employer before worker:** no live worker evidence session without a real employer's dated acceptance commitment for one verified opening.
- **No unsupported authority:** no global score, rank, recommendation, certification, verification claim, or employment prediction.
- **Worker control:** the worker can correct, omit, withhold, and locally export the record.
- **Missing stays visible:** sparse or missing evidence remains `unresolved`; the system does not fill gaps.
- **Comparable measurement:** a 10:1 claim is prohibited until the same task is measured on both sides using comparable time, cost, or review probability.
- **Consent and minimization:** the public demo uses invented records only and stores no personal data.

## 6. Success before the module closes

The core feature works at a public Primer Peldaño URL when a visitor can complete the invented demo, change visible evidence, see criterion states update deterministically, inspect provenance, correct the record, and export it locally. Automated checks must pass and the final URL must return the tested production build.

This milestone proves the interaction and technical boundary only. It does **not** prove employer demand, worker benefit, a 10:1 improvement, evidence truth, or a hire.

## 7. Kill and pivot criteria

Stop a live pilot if an employer will not accept the evidence standard before the worker invests time; if reviewers cannot apply the published criteria consistently; if a worker cannot correct or withhold the record; if evidence preparation takes longer than the path it replaces; or if success is reduced to enrollment, completion, or certificate counts.

## 8. Three-year direction

If this slice works, Primer Peldaño becomes shared infrastructure through which employers, universities, and community organizations translate existing informal work into worker-controlled evidence for real openings. Employers publish reusable criterion templates and acceptance commitments; independent reviewers audit evidence quality, appeals, labor treatment, and 90-day employment outcomes. The venture succeeds only if it expands mobility without turning candidates into unpaid test workers or reducing people to opaque scores.

## 9. Variables and decision rule

Industry, business model, location, and timeline may change. The values do not. Persist when a real outside party accepts the workflow and measured results improve; pivot when the employer gate, worker control, or comparable evidence fails. Trusted advice informs the choice, but visible results and alignment decide it.
